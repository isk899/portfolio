[ unit ] Browser RTS

The Ancient Empires

Age of Empires 1, the whole thing in one browser tab.

Hardest part

Pathfinding is A* on a grid with a smoothing pass on top, so units cut corners instead of walking the staircase a grid gives you. Enemy AI is a state machine per unit — gather, build, attack, fall back — and the maps are generated procedurally, which means the generator has to place resources somewhere that isn't unfair to one side.

[ unit ] Landing page

Porsche 911 Turbo S

Concept — not client work

A landing page built out of typography and two large photographs.

Hardest part

The page is type and two photographs, so the photographs are the entire budget. They go through an image CDN with format negotiation, which hands a modern browser AVIF instead of JPEG: the hero comes down at 207 KB for a 1600px frame, the second one at 111 KB for 1800px. The hero is eager with a high fetch priority, the second is lazy, and the document itself is 7 KB over the wire.

[ unit ] Telegram bot

@Tiktokdownload899bot

Pulls videos without watermarks out of TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

Hardest part

TikTok's CDN is blocked by my ISP, so the download path can't go out directly. Traffic runs through a local SOCKS5 proxy: yt-dlp gets its own route, while the Telegram API calls keep going out over the normal one. Keeping both paths alive in a single process, without one of them quietly timing out, took longer than the downloading did.
